Moonie's Fun Farm

Welcome to Moonie's farm! Wander around with Moonie and her barnyard friends, then head into the barn and pick a flyer to play hide and seek, count the animals, splat paint, test your memory, gobble corn, play checkers, mix up animals, color a picture, learn your ABCs with veggies, or pop bubbles at the pond.

Drive the tractor, drag the slide, trampoline and seesaw anywhere you like, kick the beach ball, shoot hoops at the barn, go fishing, snack on fruit and veggies (or feed them to your friends), or jump in the pond. Somewhere on the farm, three rubber duckies are hiding… And the animals whisper about a funny little nail somewhere in the barn…

Sound check

Tap ▶ to hear each recorded line. If it says the wrong thing, tap “Wrong”, then try “◀ clip” / “clip ▶” to borrow the clip next to it, or nudge where it starts and stops. Fixes are saved on this device. Tap “Copy report” to send me what you changed.

Move games

Walk Moonie to where you want a game, pause, then pick the game and tap “Move it here”. It faces the camera. Moves are saved on this device; tap “Copy layout” and paste it to Claude to make them permanent for everyone.
