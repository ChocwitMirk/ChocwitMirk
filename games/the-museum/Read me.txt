EYEWITNESS MUSEUM

Extract this folder and open Eyewitness Museum.html in a current Chrome or
Edge browser. Keep the HTML, JavaScript, and assets together. The museum
runs locally and does not require an internet connection.

Click the scene to look around. Use W A S D to walk, Shift to run, and Esc
to release the mouse. Use M for the map or choose a gallery from the menu.
F switches between walking and flight. In flight, Space rises and C descends.

Use Aquarium to watch four tanks up close. Next Tank changes the view.
Hide Fact lets you focus on the tanks. Filtered Flute plays throughout the
museum. Use Music to mute it. Return to Museum takes you back to your
previous position.

The original museum and its T. rex dome are the base scene. This build
uses the supplied tiger, macaw, biplane, crocodile, elephant, sports car,
eagle, Triceratops, animated Shepherd, and four fish models. The legacy
pheasant and small props remain where matching source meshes were unavailable.

XBOX CONTROLLER
Press a controller button to wake it, then press A on the title screen.
Left stick walks, right stick looks, and pressing the left stick runs.
Look toward an exhibit marker and press A to read its discovery. B returns
to the museum. With a keyboard, use E to discover and Escape to close.
View opens the map. Menu opens help. In the aquarium, A changes tanks,
X toggles the fact, Y toggles music, and B returns to the museum.
