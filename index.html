HOW TO PLAY

Select a cartridge to play. On iPhone and phones without fullscreen support, the game opens directly. In the TV arcade, use Fullscreen to expand the screen or Open Game to play on its own page.

Each cartridge loads its own game folder. Update a game by replacing the index.html inside its folder.
